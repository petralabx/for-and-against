Sponsored Products spent $1,620 for 77 orders and $1,468 in attributed sales in the 30 days to Oct 23 (110% ACOS, $21 per order on a $19 product), and $11,697 for $13,404 since April (87% ACOS). - The Scent Keywords campaign has 122 distinct search terms and **no negative keywords** after six months; the 109 terms outside the top 13 spent about $474 for 13 orders.
- One ad group mixes three scented bottles, so a "bergamot body wash" search can serve the Amber & Sandalwood bottle.
- Branded search ("for and against body wash") converts at 6.6x ROAS but has no campaign of its own; competitors (Salt & Stone video, ATTITUDE, Hempz) advertise on your detail pages and nothing defends them. ### What the table says

- **Borrowed demand.** The two Salt & Stone queries delivered 21,655 of your impressions and 10 purchases. Amazon's own "frequently bought together" on your page pairs you with their bottle, and buyers' reviews judge the product as a dupe ("not a dupe for the more posh brand", "I'd just use Nivea if not Salt and Stone"). This traffic is real but it sets the comparison frame against a $36 product.
- **Owned niches.** Santal shampoo (7 of 11 purchases), bergamot shampoo (18%), bergamot body lotion (16%), bergamot lotion (7%) and hinoki body wash (6%) are queries where the brand already takes a meaningful share. They are small (300–1,400 searches a month) but they are the organic base to build on, and they point at the scent-led, hair-and-lotion side of the range rather than the generic body wash fight.
- **The refill headroom.** "body wash refill" and "body wash refill pouch" are 8,700 searches and 636 purchases a month where you hold 2–4%; the 2L refill is also the best-converting and highest-margin product in the catalog. This is the clearest organic growth lane at the current price point.
- **The generic fight is unwinnable at this scale.** On "body wash" and "body wash women" the brand's share is 0.00–0.01%, yet these are the terms broad-match ads keep buying.
- **Funnel quality is fine when the product is in stock.** On "bergamot body wash" 74 clicks became 35 cart adds and 10 purchases (47% add-to-cart, 13.5% purchase per click), well above the query's own 18% and 7% rates — the listing converts when the shopper came for that scent.

Customer Loyalty Analytics (week 42) shows 1,733 customers, 85 of 88 weekly orders new-to-brand, a repeat purchase rate of 3.45% (3 repeat orders, down 52%), and 1,986 cart abandoners in the last 90 days. For a consumable bought every 6–8 weeks the repeat rate is the number to move; the cart-abandoner pool is immediately addressable with a Brand Tailored Promotion.
