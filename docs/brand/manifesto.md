# Brand manifesto

**Last verified:** 2026-08-05 · Links: [voice](voice.md) · [competitors](../competitors/README.md)

The founding philosophy behind For & Against, source: brand guideline decks. Use for brand-story content (About page, founder narrative, high-context creative briefs) — not a substitute for the on-voice constructions already catalogued in [voice.md](voice.md).

## Our philosophy

There is no middle ground. Not here, not now — we take sides. Not just on the world out there, but on every single ingredient in here.

We've drawn a line down the centre of our products: a statement that makes you draw your own.

For natural. Against artificial.
For confidence. Against shame.
For fair pricing. Against price gouging.

Because if you don't know what you stand for, and what you stand against, you won't just fall for anything — you'll end up with anything on your skin and in your body.

It's just that simple.

## Usage notes

- This is source brand-story language, not ready-to-ship ad copy — adapt tone per channel per [voice.md](voice.md).
- The "for X / against Y" structure is a recurring content pattern (see per-product "For / Against" ingredient framing in packaging copy) — keep that structure available when briefing product-level creative, but claims used in the "for/against" lists must still clear [claims policy](../compliance/claims.md).
