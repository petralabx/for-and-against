# Channel: Paid ads (Google + Meta)

**Last verified:** 2026-07-14 — strategy documented, campaigns not yet live

## Strategy

Google and Meta ads run against forandagainstbodycare.com, which pushes traffic to Amazon listings — capitalizing on Amazon's ranking boost for external traffic. The website is a bridge, not the primary conversion point.

## Rules

- Comparative/dupe claims in paid ads are higher-risk than organic — see [claims policy](../compliance/claims.md). "Luxury body care without the markup" is safe; naming competitors in ad copy requires case-by-case review.
- Landing pages: scent or product-line pages on Webflow with clear Amazon CTAs.
- Creative uses design tokens (`docs/design-system/tokens.css`) and scent accent colors; copy voice per [brand/voice.md](../brand/voice.md).
- Approved ad copy archives to `copy/ads/`; test results to `docs/learnings.md`.
