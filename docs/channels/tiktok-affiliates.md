# Channel: TikTok Shop + creators/affiliates

**Last verified:** 2026-07-14 — planned channel, not yet live

## Strategy

Leverage the dupe positioning with creators and affiliate partners. This is the one channel where explicit "alternative to [brand]" framing is the core content mechanic — dupe content is a native TikTok genre.

## Rules

- Creator briefs draw scent stories from [brand/scents/](../brand/scents/) and comparisons from the [dupe map](../competitors/dupe-map.md) — price deltas verified at brief time, not from memory.
- Framing: "the accessible alternative to X" / "same scent profile" — never "identical formula," never disparagement (see [claims](../compliance/claims.md)).
- FTC disclosure (#ad) mandatory in all sponsored/affiliate content.
- **UGC rights:** every piece of creator content we license gets a row in `data/creators.csv` (creator, content link, rights granted, expiry). No paid usage of content past its rights window.
