# Claims & compliance

**Last verified:** 2026-07-14 · Applies to ALL generated copy, every channel.

## Approved claims (in use on live listings)

Sulfate free · paraben free · phthalate free · dye free · vegan · cruelty free · aluminum free (deodorant) · baking soda free (deodorant) · "helps support / helps strengthen" ingredient benefits (niacinamide, panthenol, hyaluronic acid, amino acid complex, vitamin B5/E, aloe) · "we own our manufacturing facility."

Keep cosmetic-benefit phrasing ("helps," "supports," "leaves skin feeling") — never drug claims (treats, cures, heals, prevents, repairs skin damage, anti-inflammatory).

## Restricted / flagged

- **"All Natural"** — currently in live hand wash and lotion-pouch titles. High-risk: formulas contain synthetics (e.g. Sodium C14-16 Olefin Sulfonate, Polyester-11). Also off-voice. Recommend removing; do not reuse in new copy.
- **"Non-Toxic"** — in live deodorant titles. FTC scrutiny magnet; avoid in new copy.
- **"reverse-engineer premium formulas"** — in live body wash descriptions. Legal review recommended; don't spread to new copy until cleared.

## Dupe / comparative language — channel matrix

| Channel | Naming competitors | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon listings + backend keywords | **NEVER** | Policy violation, suppression risk. "$36 a bottle" without naming is the ceiling. |
| TikTok / creators / affiliates | Allowed | "Accessible alternative to X"; never "identical formula"; FTC #ad disclosure. |
| Organic social / blog / email | Allowed with care | Comparison framing OK; no disparagement, no trademark misuse in ad-like placements. |
| Google / Meta paid | Case-by-case | Trademark bidding and comparative claims reviewed individually. |
| Website | Allowed with care | Comparison content OK; avoid competitor names in metadata/SEO titles. |

## Canada (before marketplace launch)

- Health Canada Cosmetic Notification Form within 10 days of first sale
- Bilingual (EN/FR) labeling requirements; INCI naming
- Quebec retail requires French; plan for FR product pages/packaging before any CA retail push
- Recheck claim rules under Canadian law — US-approved phrasing is not automatically compliant
